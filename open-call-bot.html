WOCR open call bot

If you found WOCR-verkenning in your server logs, this page explains what it is.

Who runs it

Zhao Zhou, an artist working in new media art. It is a personal research tool, not a company or a commercial service.

What it does

It looks for open calls, residencies and grants in new media art and related fields, so I can keep track of deadlines. It reads only public pages. It does not log in, fill in forms or republish your pages.

How it behaves

  • It makes one request at a time, with at least 5 seconds between requests to the same site.
  • It visits only a small number of pages per site.
  • It checks robots.txt first and follows it.
  • It stops at once when a site answers with 403 or 429, or shows a bot check.

Want it to stop?

Add this to your robots.txt:

User-agent: WOCR-verkenning
Disallow: /

Or contact me: info@zhao-zhou.com. I will exclude your site.
